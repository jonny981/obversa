The [Jev engine](/packages/engine-jev-api) answers typed questions, but it
doesn't route work to a deliberate seat on its own. This page proposes
that routing.

Most decisions in a run are small. Is this ticket the kind the
policy answers? Has this note actually changed since the reviewer read it?
Is the reply ready to send? Asking a full agentic seat costs a turn of
inference for each one, which is why gates like these are usually left out
of a workflow rather than written into it.

A fast decision model answers that kind of question with a typed choice and
a confidence. A step would ask it first, and hand the work to the
deliberate seat only when the confidence falls under the gate, or when the
decision itself says the question needs thinking about.

The point is what becomes affordable. A typed call can run on every item.
A full agentic turn can't, so it ends up running on none of them.
