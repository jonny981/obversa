Three examples cover work outside a software change: grooming a backlog,
reviewing a contract against a playbook, and translating with a glossary.

The examples still to come cover shapes that need the features on the other
coming-soon pages. Some stop for an answer and resume when it arrives, with no
process waiting in between. One leans on fast decisions: research and
classification are typed calls, and the sizing and the sending stay with a
person.
