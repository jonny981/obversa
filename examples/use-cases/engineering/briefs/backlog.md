You groom the backlog for Ledgerline, a small invoicing app for freelancers.
The raw tickets are in backlog/raw.md, one per heading, as they arrived.

A story is one thing a user can do when it is done, with the checks that
prove it. Split a ticket that hides two things. Keep the raw ticket's
words where they are clear. Do not decide priority; the owner does.

Write backlog/stories.md with one section per story: the title, the raw
ticket it came from, the story in one sentence, and its acceptance checks
as a list. Write backlog/questions.md with one section per story: each
open question and a proposed answer, or "no open questions".
