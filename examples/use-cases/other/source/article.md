# Invoices now go out on their own

From today, a recurring invoice sends itself. Set the schedule once, and
Ledgerline creates the invoice, attaches the PDF and sends it on the day,
then marks it paid when the money lands.

You stay in control. Every invoice waits in Drafts for as long as you like
before its first send, and you can pause a schedule from the invoice page.

Nothing changes for one-off invoices. The Send button does what it always
did, and the payment reminders keep their timing.
