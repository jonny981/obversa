Translate source/article.md, a product update from Ledgerline, into French
for freelancers in France. Warm, direct, "vous". Keep the paragraphs.
Render every term in glossary/en-fr.md exactly as the glossary says, even
where another word would read more naturally, and say so in the terms
note.

The work happens in two steps and each is judged on its own, so a step is
never owed the other's result.

The first step's result is fr/article.md, holding the translation.

The second step's result is fr/terms.md, a table of the English term, where it
appears, how it was rendered, and a note where the glossary and natural French
pulled apart.
