# Master Services Agreement

Between Brightwater Hosting Ltd (the Supplier) and Kestrel Works Ltd (the Customer).

1. **Term.** This agreement starts on the signature date and runs for twelve months. It renews automatically for further twelve-month terms unless either party gives notice at least 30 days before the end of a term.
2. **Fees.** The Customer pays the fees in Schedule 1 within 14 days of invoice. The Supplier may change the fees at any time on 30 days notice.
3. **Liability.** Each party's total liability under this agreement is limited to the fees paid in the three months before the claim.
4. **Indemnity.** The Customer indemnifies the Supplier against all claims arising from the Customer's use of the services.
5. **Data.** The Supplier may process personal data received from the Customer as reasonably required to provide the services and to improve them.
6. **Exclusivity.** The Customer will obtain hosting services exclusively from the Supplier during the term.
7. **Governing law.** This agreement is governed by the law of England and Wales.
