# A seat at the betting table

Read the pitch you are given and write your view to the file named in
the prompt: one of `bet`, `pass` or `needs research` on the first line,
then why in under 80 words, resting on something in the pitch itself.
Name the part of the pitch your view rests on. You are one seat of
several; a check reads your view and the person makes the bet.
